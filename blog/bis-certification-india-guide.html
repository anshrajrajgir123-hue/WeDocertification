BIS Certification in India: Why Product Compliance Matters for Manufacturers

Comprehensive overview of Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS), ISI Mark Certification Scheme, and Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) for Indian market access.

By WeDo Industrial Compliance Team • Published on November 15, 2026 • Reading Time: 4 min read

The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) serves as India's national standards body, enforcing mandatory quality and safety certifications. Under Quality Control Orders (QCOs) issued by various ministries, marketing non-certified products in India is strictly prohibited.

1. Key BIS Certification Schemes

  • Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS - Scheme II): Mandatory for IT, telecommunications, and consumer electronic goods (laptops, mobile phones, power adapters, LED lighting, smartwatches). Operates on third-party testing in BIS-recognized NABL labs without mandatory factory audit.
  • ISI Mark Certification (Scheme I - Domestic): Applies to industrial, electrical, and consumer goods (cables, cement, steel, automotive batteries, household appliances). Involves both factory inspection and independent product testing.
  • Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS): Allows international manufacturing facilities to affix the standard ISI mark on products exported to India, requiring physical factory inspection by BIS officers.

2. Core Documentation Checklist

  • Valid test report from a BIS-recognized NABL laboratory (issued within 90 days).
  • Factory registration documents, manufacturing license, and machinery layout.
  • In-house testing facility details, equipment list, and calibration certificates.
  • Quality control staff qualifications and organizational hierarchy.
  • Trademark registration certificate or brand authorization letter.

3. 4-Step BIS Certification Roadmap

  1. Standard Identification: Determine exact Indian Standard (IS number) applicable to your product.
  2. Sample Testing: Submit representative product samples to an accredited Indian NABL laboratory.
  3. Portal Application: Upload test reports, technical files, and remit statutory government fees on Manakonline.
  4. Scrutiny & License Grant: Technical scrutiny (and physical factory audit for ISI/FMCS), followed by grant of registration number.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a BIS CRS registration valid?
CRS licenses are granted initially for 2 years and can be renewed subsequently for up to 5 years.

Can multiple models be covered under one BIS report?
Yes, series guidelines allow grouping related model variations under one lead model if key components match.
