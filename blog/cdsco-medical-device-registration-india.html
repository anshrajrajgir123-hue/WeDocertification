CDSCO Medical Device Registration in India: A Complete Guide for Manufacturers and Importers

Step-by-step regulatory roadmap under the Medical Device Rules, 2017 covering risk classifications, Form MD-14/15 import licenses, Sugam filings, and Indian Authorized Agent requirements.

By WeDo Regulatory Affairs Team • Published on November 18, 2026 • Reading Time: 4 min read

India's medical technology sector operates under mandatory statutory licensing enforced by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) under the Medical Device Rules, 2017 (MDR 2017). Every domestic manufacturer and foreign importer must secure statutory approval before commercial distribution in India.

1. Risk-Based Device Classification

Under MDR 2017, all medical equipment, in-vitro diagnostics (IVDs), and software (SaMD) are categorized into four distinct risk tiers:

  • Class A (Low Risk): Thermometers, surgical dressings, tongue depressors, manual hospital beds.
  • Class B (Low-to-Moderate Risk): Hypodermic needles, blood pressure monitors, suction equipment, surgical gloves.
  • Class C (Moderate-to-High Risk): Hemodialysis catheters, ventilators, bone fixation plates, patient monitors.
  • Class D (High Risk): Coronary stents, heart valves, implantable pacemakers, neurovascular coils.

2. Licensing Framework for Importers & Manufacturers

Foreign manufacturers must appoint an Indian Authorized Agent (IAA) holding a valid Wholesale Drug License (Form 20B/21B) to manage regulatory affairs. Import licenses are granted under Form MD-15 via application Form MD-14 on the central CDSCO Sugam portal. Domestic manufacturers file Form MD-3 (for Class A/B MD-5 license) or Form MD-7 (for Class C/D MD-9 license).

3. Essential Regulatory Dossier

  • Device Master File (DMF): Design schematics, risk management file, and biocompatibility evidence.
  • Plant Master File (PMF): Manufacturing facility layout, clean room validation, and water systems.
  • Free Sale Certificate (FSC): Issued by national authority of origin (US FDA, CE, TGA, or Health Canada).
  • Quality System Certificate: Valid ISO 13485:2016 accreditation.
  • Product Labeling & IFU: Conforming to Rule 44 with Indian Authorized Agent details.

4. 4-Step Approval Workflow

  1. Classification & Gap Assessment: Identify device code, predicate equivalence, and regulatory pathway.
  2. Dossier Compilation: Structure DMF, PMF, and validation reports into CDSCO format.
  3. Sugam Portal Filing: Submit online dossier and pay statutory government fees (BharatKosh).
  4. Query Resolution & Grant: Technical review, Subject Expert Committee (SEC) liaison if needed, and license issuance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the validity of a CDSCO Form MD-15 license?
Form MD-15 licenses remain valid perpetually, provided statutory retention fees are remitted every five years.

What is the typical approval timeline?
Class A/B applications take approximately 60 to 90 working days; Class C/D devices take 4 to 6 months.
