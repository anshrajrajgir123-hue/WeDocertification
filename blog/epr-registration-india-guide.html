EPR & Environmental Compliance

EPR Registration in India: Understanding Plastic, E-Waste and Battery Waste Compliance

A practical roadmap for Producers, Importers, and Brand Owners (PIBOs) under Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) guidelines to fulfill statutory recycling obligations.

By WeDo Environmental Affairs Team • Published on November 12, 2026 • Reading Time: 4 min read

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates that Producers, Importers, and Brand Owners (PIBOs) take financial and operational responsibility for the environmentally sound management and recycling of end-of-life products entering the Indian marketplace.

1. The Three Mandatory EPR Frameworks

  • Plastic Packaging Waste: Regulated under Plastic Waste Management Rules. Categorized into Category I (rigid plastic), Category II (flexible single/multi-layer), Category III (multi-layered plastic packaging), and Category IV (compostable plastic).
  • E-Waste Management: Enforces mandatory collection and recycling targets for consumer electronics, IT equipment, telecommunications devices, and solar photovoltaic panels.
  • Battery Waste Management: Encompasses portable batteries, electric vehicle (EV) traction batteries, automotive batteries, and industrial energy storage systems.

2. Core Statutory Obligations for PIBOs

  • Mandatory central registration on the CPCB centralized EPR web portal.
  • Quantitative tracking of packaging material and products imported or manufactured.
  • Procurement of verifiable EPR Recycling Credits from CPCB-registered recyclers.
  • Filing annual statutory returns before the mandatory June 30 compliance deadline.

3. 4-Step EPR Compliance Roadmap

  1. Material Audit & Target Modeling: Calculate annual baseline generation and statutory recycling targets.
  2. CPCB Portal Submission: Upload company documentation, IEC, GST, and product sales data.
  3. EPR Certificate Grant: CPCB technical evaluation and issuance of formal EPR Registration Certificate.
  4. Credit Fulfillment: Partner with verified recyclers to settle annual credit obligations on the portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the penalties for non-compliance?
Failure to register or fulfill targets attracts Environmental Compensation (EC) fines and customs consignment holds.

How fast can an EPR certificate be issued?
Standard CPCB portal verification and approval takes between 20 to 30 working days with complete documentation.
