USFDA 510(k) Submission: What Medical Device Manufacturers Should Understand Before Entering the US Market

Navigating FDA Premarket Notification pathways, predicate device equivalence, eSTAR submissions, biocompatibility testing, and FDA review milestones.

By WeDo US Regulatory Practice • Published on October 28, 2026 • Reading Time: 4 min read

To commercialize a medical device in the United States, manufacturers must demonstrate to the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) that the device is at least as safe and effective as—and substantially equivalent to—a legally marketed predicate device through a 510(k) Premarket Notification.

1. Determining 510(k) Applicability

Under FDA classification, medical devices fall into three categories: Class I (General Controls - mostly 510(k) exempt), Class II (Special Controls - standard 510(k) clearance pathway), and Class III (Premarket Approval - PMA for high-risk life-sustaining systems).

2. Core Components of a 510(k) Dossier

  • Predicate Device Identification: Selecting cleared K-number predicates with matching intended use and technological characteristics.
  • Substantial Equivalence (SE) Comparison: Side-by-side engineering table highlighting design, materials, and operational parity.
  • Performance Testing Benchmarks: Mechanical bench testing, electrical safety & EMC (IEC 60601-1), software validation, and sterility assurance.
  • Biocompatibility Evaluation: Cytotoxicity, sensitization, and irritation testing conducted under ISO 10993 standards.
  • Labeling & IFU: Complete operator manuals, contraindications, and device markings.

3. 4-Step FDA 510(k) Clearance Roadmap

  1. Predicate Selection & Strategy: Perform regulatory analysis to confirm substantial equivalence feasibility.
  2. eSTAR Dossier Assembly: Compile all testing data into the FDA's mandatory digital eSTAR submission format.
  3. User Fee Remittance: Pay MDUFA statutory user fee (applying for Small Business Determination fee relief if eligible).
  4. FDA Review & Clearance: 15-day Acceptance Review, 60-day Substantive Review (RTA), and final 510(k) Clearance Order.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Small Business Determination (SBD) discount?
Qualifying businesses with gross receipts under $100M receive substantial fee discounts (up to 75% reduction on 510k user fees).

How long does FDA 510(k) review take?
Standard FDA review clock is 90 calendar days, excluding any time the applicant takes to respond to Additional Information (AI) requests.
