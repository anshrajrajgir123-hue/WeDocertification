Food & FSSAI

FSSAI License in India: What Food Businesses Should Know Before Starting Operations

Clear breakdown of FSSAI Basic, State, and Central licensing categories, documentation, hygiene criteria, and FoSCoS portal compliance for food and nutraceuticals.

By WeDo Food Regulatory Advisory • Published on November 10, 2026 • Reading Time: 4 min read

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) regulates food manufacturing, processing, storage, distribution, and importation under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. Operating any food business without a valid 14-digit FSSAI license number is a punishable statutory offense.

1. The Three Tiers of FSSAI Licensing

  • Basic FSSAI Registration: For petty food manufacturers, small retailers, and mobile food vendors with annual turnover up to ₹12 Lakhs.
  • State FSSAI License: For mid-sized food processing units, restaurants, cold storages, and distributors with annual turnover between ₹12 Lakhs and ₹20 Crores.
  • Central FSSAI License: Mandatory for large manufacturers (turnover > ₹20 Crores), 100% Export Oriented Units (EOUs), e-commerce food platforms, proprietary food manufacturers, and all food importers.

2. Mandatory Documentation Requirements

  • Blueprint or layout plan of the processing facility with dimensions and machinery placement.
  • List of plant equipment, installed horsepower capacity, and production flow charts.
  • Potable water testing report from an accredited NABL testing laboratory.
  • Food Safety Management System (FSMS) plan or accredited ISO 22000 / HACCP certificate.
  • Food Safety Supervisor (FoSTaC) training certificate.

3. 4-Step FoSCoS Portal Application Process

  1. Category & Kind of Business (KoB) Mapping: Select exact Indian food categories and manufacturing sub-types.
  2. Dossier Preparation: Compile FSMS manual, premises ownership proof, and machinery lists.
  3. Online Submission: Upload files and pay statutory fees through the FoSCoS portal.
  4. Inspection & License Grant: Food Safety Officer (FSO) premises inspection and digital license issuance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum tenure for an FSSAI license?
FBOs can select license tenures ranging from 1 to 5 years, with renewal applications submitted 30 days before expiry.

Is FSSAI mandatory for imported packaged foods?
Yes, all imported food consignments must hold a Central FSSAI Import License for customs clearance.
