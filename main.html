About WeDo

A trusted compliance and certification partner

WeDo Certification & Services is an accredited compliance management and industrial testing consultancy headquartered in Noida & Delhi. Founded with a vision to make Indian regulatory approvals straightforward, reliable, and transparent, we specialize in turnkey liaisoning with government authorities including the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), Wireless Planning & Coordination (WPC), Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE), and Ministry of Environment (EPR). Our seasoned team of regulatory engineers and technical auditors partners with leading NABL-accredited laboratories to deliver dependable testing, flawless documentation, and rapid certificate issuance for global and domestic enterprises.

  • Client-focused approach from first call to certificate
  • Support across BIS, WPC, BEE, EPR, G-Mark, CE, ISO, FSSAI and more
  • Transparent process and timelines
