Who We Are

A Premier Regulatory Advisory Firm

Founded by Nishant Sharma, WeDo Certification & Services was built to provide clarity, reliability, and speed in an increasingly complex regulatory landscape. We specialize in government liaison, testing coordination, and statutory compliance for a wide spectrum of product categories.

From consumer electronics requiring BIS CRS registration to heavy machinery seeking the ISI Mark, environmental EPR obligations, and wireless products needing WPC ETA clearance, our team manages the entire lifecycle.

  • Expert Technical Liaison: Deep understanding of Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY), CPCB, DoT/TEC, and BEE.
  • NABL Laboratory Network: Direct coordination with premier accredited labs ensuring accurate sample testing without procedural delays.
  • End-to-End Handholding: From pre-audit documentation scrutiny, factory inspection readiness, application submission, query redressal, to final grant.
