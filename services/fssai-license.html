Required Documents & Checklist

Applicants must assemble the following standard documentation. Our consultants provide ready-to-use templates and assist in complete dossier compilation:

  • ✓ Manufacturing premises ownership deed / lease agreement and factory registration license
  • ✓ Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) appointment deed (for foreign manufacturers)
  • ✓ Plant layout blueprint detailing manufacturing, QC, raw material, and finished goods zones
  • ✓ Equipment list, calibration certificates, and testing instrument records
  • ✓ Quality Management System manual, process flowcharts, and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
  • ✓ Product technical specifications, bill of materials (BOM), user manual, and marking artwork
  • ✓ Test reports from accredited NABL / ISO 17025 testing laboratories (if applicable)
